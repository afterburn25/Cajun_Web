CajunWebPro — choose ONE download for your purpose

FULL-PROJECT is the development/project archive. It includes site/, docs/, tests/,
tools/, brand/, foundation/ and CI. Do not extract it wholesale into a public
website directory. Read docs/DIRECTORY_STRUCTURE.md and the working agreement.

COMPLETE-UPLOAD is for a fresh website. Extract its CONTENTS directly into the
intended website directory. There is no site/, version/ or upload/ wrapper.
Keep its organized subdirectories. Open install.php over HTTPS, fill the one-page
form and select Install. Setup is keyless. Do not use setup on an installed site.

UPDATE is for a recognized installed 1.0.8 through 1.10.0 website. Back up the
website files, database, and configured private storage before starting. Extract
the update into the existing website directory; it creates only the new staging
directory cwp-update-1.11.0/. Do not copy its site/ payload over the live site.
Do not overwrite an existing different staging directory. Open
cwp-update-1.11.0/owner-update.php over HTTPS and authenticate as the owner.
Confirm your recoverable backup, inspect, then apply. Keep the page and staging
files available to retry or recover if interrupted. Customized conflicting files
stop the update before activation; reconcile those using your backup. Unknown
files are preserved. The temporary maintenance policy protects the site while
changes are incomplete. Recovery copies/journal stay under your configured
private storage in structure-1.11.0/. Configuration, keys, database and private
files are never replaced or reset by this structure update.

After verifying the website, administration, portal, callbacks and private files,
remove the exact cwp-update-1.11.0 staging directory using your hosting panel.
Keep recovery copies with your backup. Recovery after application changes may
require manual review; never restore a database independently of its backup set.

These are alternatives, not sequential patches. No customer CLI/Composer/Node
service is required. No automatic hosting deployment is performed.
See the release manifests, SHA256SUMS.txt and migration instructions.
