# Native registration prerequisites and proposed interfaces

This is a proposed integration contract for the pure [registration draft](TWENTYI_DOMAIN_REGISTRATION_CONTRACT.md), based on a63. None of the shared edits below is applied. No generic sender, pricing engine, invoice generator or automatic registration is introduced.

## Exact provider input still needed

1. Registration term field, units, required/optional rules and defaults for addDomain, or a separately documented replacement request that binds the selected registration term.
2. Exact addDomain response envelope, field types and semantics: purchase acceptance versus allocated identity versus completed registration; finite refusal/unknown behavior. A true renewal response and a transfer result object cannot fill this gap.
3. For each offered initial TLD, complete registration-specific contact/extension requirements, accepted enum values, nameserver constraints, privacy applicability and registrant agreements. The contact-update mappings alone do not establish registration requirements.
4. A source-backed/current-account acceptance path for selected provider cost and account eligibility. Automatic price lookup remains unavailable; manual verified provider cost may be an explicit owner approval fact, never inferred from the retail price or the renewal-only premium servicePrice endpoint.

The documented GET/domain-period data establishes registration year options only. Reuse current saved registration-period observations with exact account/revision/key, latest-intent and audit-order integrity; this does not define how to send years to addDomain, price, availability or registry entitlement. Existing NDJSON domain search suggestions likewise cannot prove purchase availability. These are distinct source gaps, not substitutes for unfinished engineering.

## Smallest useful native product flow

A direct client selects an owner-offered ASCII domain, a requested term, explicit privacy choice and private contact. The owner reviews registry requirements and offers one existing positive one-time USD service agreement. Its immutable registration binding names the exact draft revision/fingerprint, contact fingerprint, requested term, privacy and fee. The client sees those exact terms before consent and payment instructions. Contact edits, changed years/privacy, expiry or changed client require new review/consent; old offers remain historical and cannot be accepted from captured generic agreement URLs. The existing recurring and hosting agreement paths stay intact.

Reuse the current service-plan/agreement event model, with a separate registration-purpose binding and exclusion from generic hosting preparation. Do not change ServiceOrderDocument into a generic commerce document. Support sessions cannot select or consent on a client's behalf. A .ca record requires the actual agreement text/version, current direct-client consent event and exact domain binding, independently of ordinary retail consent.

The owner explicitly allocates an ordinary unpaid invoice before any payment request, sandbox attempt or receipt. The invoice/client/USD amount/origin must equal accepted terms. Display immutable domain/term/privacy/fee purpose before payment instructions in both statement and project views. Reuse the a63 paid-renewal evidence requirements: exact receipt/audit correspondence and insertion order, permanent reservation, exclusion of recurring invoices and orphan sandbox/legacy evidence, and shared-lock atomic consumption across hosting, renewal and registration. A receipt or active agreement by itself is not payment-purpose evidence.

Only after verified paid funding and resolved provider contracts may a current owner approve the exact provider charge. Owner confirmation separately names eligibility, provider cost, balance debit, exact term/privacy and registry requirements. Retain current client/account/revision/raw-key fingerprint plus sealed-key identity; recheck before credential/private-contact access and before/after the one transport call. No transaction spans network.

Use the existing ProviderGateway and OperationLedger once, with an immutable registration approval and one idempotency key. A failed/unfinished/unknown outcome never permits a new nonce or domain reassignment to bypass the canonical-domain fence. Current registrar absence does not prove a previous charge failed. An acknowledged allocated ID, if documented, is reconciled through exact identity; none of that response grammar is implemented here.

## Proposed shared seams (review before application)

- New owned native modules: `site/app/providers/domain_registrations{,_document,_storage,_views,_routes}.php`, `site/database/schema/domain_registrations_schema.php`, dedicated admin controller and portal view. Prepare only through explicit owner action; genuinely never-prepared optional history must not break existing requests/invoices. Established lost history must refuse.
- New pure activation component, only after source gaps close: a dedicated RegistrationRequestContext holding immutable operation/account/order/document fingerprint and a protected private-field resolver. No contacts in ProviderOperation.params, scope audit text or generic ledger display; encrypted contact bytes stay in the owned application document.
- Preserve `TwentyIOperationAdapter::__construct(?callable $mailboxPassword=null)` argument1. Proposed new final argument2 is a dedicated typed RegistrationRequestContext|null. Preserve `TwentyIRequestBuilder(register, mailboxPassword)` argument1/2; proposed final argument3 is the same typed context. It must be bound to exact domains.register and immutable order/scope, never supplied from posted JSON. These signatures are proposals only; actual final integration needs source review.
- At adapter/builder admission, mismatch or absent registration context refuses before private resolution or provider credential access. The protected resolver rechecks current server-side authorization and returns the exact canonical private body whose digest is in the approved scope; body construction is impossible until the term contract is known. Credential-owned and contact-owned response redaction precedes operation-specific classification.
- FixedHttpTransport may admit only exact canonical POST/reseller/*/addDomain with the dedicated closed body contract; no arbitrary registrar URL/body. Keep register shapeComplete:false and generic approval disabled until a complete native route, paid funding, schema, contract and tests are reviewed. Mailbox/ForceSSL/cloud/renewal behavior remains unchanged.
- Add reciprocal `domain_registrations_exclude_invoice(int $invoice):void` checks under the existing staff mutex to both hosting and renewal admission; registration checks both existing lanes. Payment-request and manual receipt/reversal hooks validate permanent purpose before mutation. No optional feature requires provider settings for unrelated invoices.
- Route dedicated registration actions after existing authentication/CSRF checks; maintain existing permission/support guards and all20i navigation. Client GET renders saved data and never sends, decrypts a provider key or prepares storage. Raw contact is visible only through an explicitly authorized editor.
- Existing request/approval/audit/count patterns remain append-only. Before any indexed invoice/domain lookup, authenticate canonical encrypted-document contents and keyed proof against every duplicated index; reconcile independent totals and all audit/ledger witnesses. Page bounded scans from an unconditional first page to include empty/space-trimmed corrupt IDs. Compare latest source sequence against exact paired start-audit order; no older success revival.
- Final tables/columns/indexes are reviewed against both MySQL8.4 and MariaDB10.11. New files and exact mirrors, private Apache paths and installer inventory are integrated sequentially by root.

## Required evidence before native enablement

Pure coverage must prove exact documented term/body/response and private-contact/credential redaction, in addition to this draft's252 cases. A native four-way matrix must test real direct-client consent, prepayment allocation, invoice purpose, receipt/audit ordering, reciprocal funding exclusion, malformed/stale captured forms, optional-family compatibility, indexed-row plus recomputed-audit tampering with original keyed proof, failed/unfinished source reorder, key rotation/restoration, client reassignment, actual result-write interruption and separate-process mutex contention. Refusals must occur outside caller transactions, with exact reasons and unchanged DNS/send/approval counts.

Packaged HTTP/Chromium uses a dedicated owner and independent clients, real shared cooldowns and original history preservation. Capture owner/client desktop/mobile purpose and contact-review screens with escaped synthetic PII, explicit prices and consent, plus accepted/unknown results according to the verified contract. Do not put real contacts/secrets in screenshots or artifacts. Preserve all predecessor fixtures, commands and report sets; one root-coordinated combined full run follows focused acceptance.

This checkpoint implements the reusable pure records and testable refusal boundary. Protected native storage, agreement/invoice-purpose integration, owner/client UI, current authority, single dispatch and reconciliation remain engineering work. The four provider-input items above remain separate factual gaps.
