## Locked visual direction

Preserve the composition and visual character of the approved image:

1. **Palette/materials:** deep navy and royal-blue depth, restrained blue lighting, metallic-gold highlights and selected states, thin blue/gold panel borders, near-white readable body text, polished dimensional controls. Do not flatten this into a generic gray/white template, mute the palette, add flying/disintegrating pixels, or overwhelm it with neon effects.
2. **Header:** prominent left brand placement, gold curved accent, service/help search, notification bell, and client avatar/name/account menu. The interface brand is CajunWebPro, without `.com`. Retain the existing approved production horizontal logo and exact fleur-de-lis favicon; generated lettering is not permission to replace those original assets.
3. **Left navigation:** persistent desktop sidebar with clear icons, gold-highlighted active selection, and the support/help panel near the bottom. Reference categories are Dashboard, My Services, Domains, Emails, Databases, SSL & Security, Billing & Invoices, Support Tickets, My Projects, Account Settings, and Knowledge Base. Scope available entries to the client and implemented capabilities; this inventory is a navigation target, not proof every section has shipped.
4. **Welcome banner:** a wide center-column banner with Louisiana bayou/cypress sunset imagery and an elegant white/gold two-line headline: “Your Online Success Starts Here.” Retain the image/text balance, dark readable overlay, and personalized welcome. Use an approved/licensed decorative source; do not use the full dashboard screenshot as the website.
5. **Overview tiles:** four aligned desktop metric tiles for hosting packages, domains, email accounts, and unpaid invoices, with gold icons, clear counts, secondary links, and an attention treatment for balances requiring action.
6. **Primary content:** a dominant Your Services table with service thumbnail/name, type, meaningful status, renewal information when known, and scoped Manage actions. Keep information hierarchy, spacing, borders, and row controls close to the reference.
7. **Right column:** Account Overview, Quick Actions, and Recent Support Tickets as distinct stacked cards. Match the reference's visual density without squeezing or clipping content.
8. **Lower center:** Latest Invoices and Announcements panels side by side on sufficiently wide screens, with complete visible headings and view-all controls.
9. **Footer:** full-width navy-and-gold brand strip, compact brand placement on the left and the distinctive closing brand treatment on the right. Keep it visible in full-page acceptance captures; do not crop the page to hide missing sections.

Match this design when implementing client service, domain, server, invoice, project and support pages, carrying over its component styling. The public homepage is not being redesigned by this approval. This is the client portal, not a replacement administration theme or a change to Website_Core.

## Real data and functional boundaries

The screenshot's people, companies, domains, counts, amounts, dates, invoice IDs, ticket IDs, renewal dates, support activity, offers, and statuses are illustrative. They are not seed data for production, authorized pricing, promises, or evidence of successful operations.

- Render the signed-in client's actual records only. No reseller-wide balances, other clients' services, internal notes, API credentials, or administrator webmail.
- “Verified Account,” “Active,” and “All systems operational” require the corresponding verified evidence. An API connection check does not prove live service health. Show not checked, unavailable, stale, pending or attention states honestly rather than a fabricated green badge.
- The screenshot's referral reward, product-launch announcements, and support wording are not approval of new commercial terms or service-level promises. Announcements must come from authorized content management.
- Counts must use the actual data available with honest coverage. No available data is not necessarily zero. Hosted names and registered domains remain distinct; the layout does not change ownership rules.
- Quick actions for ordering, domains, mailboxes, databases and SSL must reach real, permission-checked workflows. Hide unavailable actions or disable them with a clear explanation; never show a pretend successful operation, broken destination, or provider purchase without existing consent/eligibility.
- “Emails” means the client's own permitted hosting email services. It does not expose the agency's private administrator inbox.
- Preserve existing project milestones, messages, approvals, private files and website deliveries while adopting the approved portal shell. Do not delete working features because only their navigation entry is visible in this dashboard image.
