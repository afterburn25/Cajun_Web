## Why this completion boundary is usable

The production gateway calls its synchronous adapter before recording the terminal ledger result. A durable terminal ledger therefore establishes that this gateway attempt reached its record boundary. It does not establish process exit or remote settlement. A delayed same-key gateway entry replays the recorded result without re-entering the adapter. A delayed original local-terminal insert cannot overwrite the recovered witness's primary key. The owning staff mutex serializes normal dispatch and recovery; recovery never calls a provider reader, writer, credential opener or gateway.

This relies on the retained database ledger as completion evidence. Before the dedicated witness exists, the ledger's result has no separate finish HMAC or audit. The original request proof authenticates the approval and request input, not arbitrary changes to a result row before review. This feature is not recovery from arbitrary database corruption and cannot authenticate a forged plausible completion independently of the database. Missing/inflight ledger, original-owner changes, revoked approval, changed assignment/account and damaged history remain unresolved. General operator takeover and broad crash recovery are still unfinished.
