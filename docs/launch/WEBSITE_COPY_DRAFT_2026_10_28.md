## Service-description drafts

Use these only for services the owner confirms are offered. Any included limits, price, turnaround and ongoing responsibilities must be supplied before a product is published.

| Service | Draft summary |
|---|---|
| Business website design | A website organized around your business, services and customer journey, with responsive layouts and the features agreed in your project scope. |
| Website redesign | Review your existing content and customer journey, then plan a redesign around the information and functionality your business needs. |
| Custom web applications | Plan a tailored interface or workflow for a defined business need. Scope, integrations, access and delivery stages are agreed before work begins. |
| Website care | Agree on a defined amount of ongoing website work, a service period and a clear process for requesting changes. Included work and response expectations are documented separately. |
