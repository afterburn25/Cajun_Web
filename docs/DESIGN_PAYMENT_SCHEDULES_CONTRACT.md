## Immutable financial record

`DesignPaymentSchedule::fromArray()` accepts exactly the documented ordered fields. Its format1 snapshot contains a caller-generated32-hex ID, positive client/project/proposal IDs and proposal version, a64-hex proposal-content digest, USD, a visible title, exact terms, ordered item lines and ordered payment stages. IDs/digests are bindings to be verified by the later native adapter, not evidence of authorization or consent supplied by this class. No credentials, email address, card data, payment nonce, provider result or secret belongs in this snapshot.

At most50 item lines use unique32-hex IDs, a visible description, quantity1–100, positive unit cents, nonnegative discount cents no larger than the line subtotal, and nonnegative explicitly approved tax cents. No automatic tax rate, jurisdiction, discount policy or recurring consent is inferred. Each line total and the sum must be positive and at most99,999,999 cents, matching existing invoices. Displayed aggregate subtotal, discounts and tax are bounded to the same maximum before addition. All money, counts and basis points are integers; floats, numeric strings, booleans and overflow are rejected. USD is the existing supported currency, not a claim that other currencies are implemented.

At most32 stages retain unique32-hex identities, title, optional real ISO due date, kind, calculation, value and optional milestone32-hex ID. A full-payment schedule is exactly one `full`/`remainder` stage. A split schedule has optional first `deposit`, zero or more `milestone` stages and one last `final`/`remainder` stage. All nonfinal installments use fixed cents or1–10,000 basis points of the immutable total; percentages round half up to a cent using integer arithmetic. Every installment must be positive, and the final amount is the positive exact remainder. Reject over-allocation, zero rounded amounts and totals that leave no final balance. Milestone stages require a milestone ID; other kinds prohibit it. Native issuance must verify the milestone belongs to the current authorized project, while later milestone state changes cannot rewrite accepted financial terms.

The computed projection contains item subtotal/discount/tax/total, each stage amount and the remaining **scheduled** amount after that stage. It is not a paid balance or receipt. Allocation never infers which installment is collectible, overdue, completed or paid from the current date, project stage or client approval. Actual ledger receipts will supply those facts separately. Due dates are optional contractual facts and must not be invented from stage ordering.

The canonical JSON fingerprint covers the full binding, terms, prices, calculation rules, order and dates. Literal UTF-8 is preserved; no trimming, truncation or HTML interpretation occurs. Text controls/invalid UTF-8 and unknown fields refuse. The whole encoded record is capped at128KiB. A rehydrated record must verify against its stored fingerprint in the native layer; editing any term produces a new revision and new client consent.

## Acceptance and boundaries

Pure tests must cover a design-only full payment, fixed and percentage deposits, milestone/final allocations, half-cent rounding, exact largest supported total, distribution conservation, immutable copies/fingerprints, wrong field types/order/IDs, duplicate lines/stages, invalid dates/text, under/over-allocation, input size, and tampered amount/currency/binding. Run both default and no-ini PHP8.2/8.4 modes with network disabled. These tests do not establish storage, HTTP/browser, gateway or production acceptance.

Native and interface follow-ons must exercise current ownership/authority, proposal and milestone changes, competing edits/consents/issuance, original retries, strict audit rollback, current balances, revoked access, cross-client isolation, update/recovery and actual desktop/mobile rendering. Gateway follow-ons retain declined/unknown/concurrent/reconciliation/refund and exact merchant/amount/currency checks. Existing release, independent review, immutable publication and deployment rules remain.
