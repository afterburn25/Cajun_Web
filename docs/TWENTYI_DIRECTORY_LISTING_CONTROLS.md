## Narrow durable recovery

The finished-ledger recovery architecture is retained from the accepted Force SSL implementation. Only the original approving owner, unchanged authentication identity, unrevoked grant and valid current assignment/account can reconstruct a missing local terminal witness. The exact stored consumed request and finished single-attempt ledger digest are reviewed and rechecked under the owning mutex and transaction. Recovery changes no original ledger, grant, request or sharing evidence and performs no provider read/write. It leaves the effect uncertain until a separately requested fresh GET.

Missing/claimed/partial/reconciled or success-like ledger rows remain ineligible. Lost previously committed witnesses are history damage, detected by their independent counters and audits; the recovery action cannot repair them. The finished shared ledger has no independent finish HMAC before the dedicated witness exists. Recovery therefore trusts that durable database completion boundary, not arbitrary forged plausible results. General interruption recovery and remote-settlement proof remain unavailable. A delayed same-key gateway entry cannot send again; a delayed terminal insertion cannot replace a recovered primary-key witness.
