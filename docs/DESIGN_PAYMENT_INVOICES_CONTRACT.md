# Design payment obligations and invoice issuance

This contract extends native financial offers/consent at `26e1008c89e58d00709b6183bc1255be57152aab` in draft#114, before invoice implementation. It preserves [financial storage](DESIGN_PAYMENT_STORAGE_CONTRACT.md), the exact schedule model, current client consent and existing invoice payment ledger. Complete20i remains Devin-owned. No provider, checkout or automatic charge is part of this increment.

## Required result

Authorized staff may explicitly issue one existing-portal USD invoice for an accepted payment stage: full service, deposit, milestone or final remainder. The invoice amount comes from the accepted immutable schedule's calculated stage, never a submitted amount or the full quote repeated for every installment. The stage's agreed due date and source terms remain available in financial history. Issuing an invoice records an obligation, not payment, delivery, milestone completion or project progress. Dates and production status do not automatically issue invoices.

After the first invoice, the complete accepted financial schedule is frozen. Publishing replacement terms or withdrawing that schedule refuses, including after payment or reversal. Amendments, credits, cancellations and replacement invoices require a separate reviewed policy; this increment does not silently substitute those operations. Existing unrelated invoices remain independent and are never inferred to be payments or automatically adopted into a stage.

## Durable binding and transaction

Add an explicit owner-prepared `design_payment_invoices` mapping with its own storage version. Each row binds project/client, accepted schedule and exact fingerprint, stage ID, accepted consent event, issued event, existing invoice ID, current staff actor, original request fingerprint and immutable invoice fingerprint. Unique constraints enforce one invoice per schedule/stage, one mapping per invoice and one mapping per request/event. Restrictive foreign keys retain the related project/client/events/invoice/actor. Unknown schema/version, incompatible columns/indexes/relationships and unrecognized populated data refuse without adoption. Existing consent-only storage upgrades additively; neither old invoices nor customer rows are rewritten. Setup requires current owner password, payment and client permissions, ready financial storage and the existing invoice ledger.

`design_payments_issue(project, client, request, expected, schedule, fingerprint, stage)` runs under the shared staff mutex and one transaction. Reject inherited transactions. Recheck current financial/client permissions, active client, current project ownership, intact financial history, exact accepted schedule and consent, and latest approved source proposal/current referenced milestones before a new invoice. Validate the caller's original expected financial head. Read the stage and integer amount from the accepted model. Insert the existing `studio_invoices` row, immutable `issued` financial event, stage/invoice mapping, financial head and strict audit atomically. Any failure rolls back all new rows. No supplied arbitrary invoice details, amount or gateway result may replace the accepted values.

The invoice title is the accepted stage title; instructions identify the financial schedule/stage and agreed due date, with complete terms available in the retained financial offer. Its reference is unique and generated by the application. Preserve a canonical fingerprint covering the actual invoice's ID, project, reference, title, amount, currency, instructions and creation time. Mutable payment status, paid note and payment timestamp are excluded. Every retained issued event names the exact accepted consent, stage allocation and immutable invoice snapshot; the mapping must agree with that evidence. Stage titles/terms are escaped only when rendering, never silently normalized into a different accepted value.

Original identical request retries return the same invoice after current authorization/ownership and complete history/binding validation, even after a later source-proposal change. Reusing a request for another actor, project, client, expected head, stage or schedule refuses. A different request for an already-issued stage refuses; two competing requests cannot produce two invoices. Map or event loss must never reopen an obligation for reissue. The immutable chain and mapping cross-check both directions, and the existing head anchor detects missing tail history. These checks detect partial corruption; they do not claim to defeat a privileged complete database rollback/rewrite.

## Existing invoice ledger and reads

All financial history validates issued event transitions, source consent, stages, map cardinality, invoice ownership and immutable invoice bytes. A missing map, extra map, missing invoice, changed amount/currency/reference/title/instructions/creation time or invalid invoice state is unavailable, never zero due or an unissued stage. Existing invoice/payment reads must verify the binding before accepting the first or a subsequent receipt, preparing payment requests or changing recorded payment state. Payment-ledger balances remain the authority for actual paid and outstanding cents; schedule allocations remain agreed amounts. Receipt reversals reopen the same invoice and cannot allocate a replacement. There is no refund implementation here.

Staff/client projections show each agreed stage's invoice when issued, with current ledger balance obtained through the existing authorized invoice path. Client ownership is checked against both the project and retained client binding; only the authorized client can follow the invoice link. Scoped support remains read-only, with current access and existing audit. No staff-only actor/request metadata or private receipt notes are added to the client view. Passive reads must preserve session idle deadlines.
