## Verified defects and correction

The previous replacement-contract reader stopped at the first unpaid period. A partial receipt on that period, or an already-paid later period, could therefore leave the original full remaining ARB amount scheduled. The reader now validates every remaining installment under the existing transaction and permits only a completely paid consecutive prefix followed by pristine issued balances. It checks the contract again at reservation and after retiring the predecessor.

Payments can also commit while provider creation or first readback is in flight. Replacement finalization now verifies the proposed contract against its original signed period offset and rechecks current balances. An obsolete known candidate is retained with its predecessor history and `retirement_pending`, then independently verified and canceled. Cancellation requires terminal readback; uncertain outcomes retain that state for the scheduler or authenticated owner, preventing another create.

Independent review also identified the corresponding initial-enrollment race. Both initial callers now recheck authoritative enrollment eligibility inside finalization for a creating or null-ID reservation. Partial receipts, a fully paid installment, and completion of a one-time payment prepared before reservation all retire the known full-contract candidate. Lost initial create responses retain the unresolved reservation. The owner can recover the verified existing candidate without creating another. An injected finalization audit failure reproduced another 4/22 failures: the fallback retained a known ID as ordinary review, allowing later owner reconciliation to overlook the cancellation obligation. The fallback now atomically retains that ID with pending retirement; the targeted probe passes 22/22, and the final suite includes both caller variants. Established known-ID reconciliation remains distinct so legitimate historical ARB receipts do not trigger this new initial-admission check.

Lifecycle replays, generic review marking, settlement and verified refund/void processing preserve a pending cancellation. Financial reversal records still commit; they cannot erase the outstanding gateway retirement. Scheduler and owner retries verify the retained contract before closing it. The administrator's recovery list now includes pending cancellation; its former filter hid these rows even though the recovery function was available. The portal explains pending cancellation and preserves the duplicate-enrollment restriction. Its suspended-result message no longer incorrectly claims that a candidate was never created. License identity, domains, payment history and permanent payoff remain protected.

The shared retirement helper retains its `replacement_retire` function name but also handles the newly created initial candidate. No gateway credentials, schema version, license rights or production activation settings change.
