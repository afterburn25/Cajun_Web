# Initial financing enrollment after verified payment

Date: 2026-10-28 (America/Chicago). Branch: `codex/finance-initial-payment-enrollment`; base [PR #295](https://github.com/afterburn25/Cajun_Web/pull/295), `27d7b9e87d912126a877ffbf906ca8af2e035a16`. The enclosing correction commit identifies the reviewed source; neither reserved implementation branch is modified.

## Failure and correction

The portal displayed automatic installment enrollment after a nonzero initial invoice was marked paid. The client handler unconditionally rejected all nonzero initial payments before reading the invoice. This prevented verified manual/external receipts and recovered initial payments from reaching enrollment when no subscription existed.

The handler now admits signed zero-down agreements or fully paid initial invoices backed by the prepared ledger. It checks the signed fingerprint and immutable agreement fields, exact invoice amount/currency, customer/project, the retained agreement ID in invoice instructions, and the original ledger obligation hash and balance. A display-only paid flag, partial payment, reversal, substituted invoice or unavailable ledger cannot authorize enrollment.

Current client authentication and agreement/license/project ownership are checked again within the existing subscription reservation mutex and SQL transaction. The current financing plan, running canonical schedule and finite future contract must still match. Existing reservations remain exclusive; a paid-off timestamp or state always refuses new installments. No payment, license state, domain or commercial-rights row is changed by admission. The verified-profile checkout path retains its separate behavior, including its sandbox semantics. Replacement enrollment, settlement and payoff services are unchanged.

The portal uses the same ledger-backed initial-payment reader and suppresses initial-payment/enrollment controls for paid-off plans. Existing first-future-period and finite-count policy remains: this correction does not silently enroll elapsed installments or replace an existing subscription.
